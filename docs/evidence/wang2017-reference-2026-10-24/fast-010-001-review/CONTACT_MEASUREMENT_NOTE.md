# Particle-centre motion and measured strip descent

These observables use different physical points. The maximum native VTK particle-centre displacement is59.128931mm, on tracked particle509. The CSV59.535670mm is the width-weighted descent of six particle top surfaces. Frozen boundary.lisp defines top-point as centre plus half the current domain height. Each top-surface descent therefore equals centre descent plus half the domain-height reduction; domain compression can make surface descent exceed every particle-centre displacement norm.

Final strip mean centre descent 57.581633873mm + mean half-height reduction 1.954035690mm = mean surface descent 59.535669563mm. Reconstruction from saved coordinates/size_y matches the CSV over all61states to maximum 5.211e-15m; individual maximum-gap reconstruction differs by at most 1.190e-15m. The script also checks these identities within1e-12m, compatible with CSV serialization precision. These are measurement identities, not scientific-validation tests. Exact frozen source hashes, individual particle values and all61identity records are in completed-trial-review.json/contactGeometryIdentity. No solver or physics was changed.
